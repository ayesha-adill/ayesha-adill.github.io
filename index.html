Biofill — Automated Syringe-Filling Device — Inventor, Arduino (C++), FEA, Excel, 3D Printing

Capstone Project · 2026 · Team of 4 · Patent Pending

What?

  • Motor-actuated table-lift syringe-filling machine for Tessella Biosciences
  • Fills 2–10 mL syringes with hydrogel inside a biosafety cabinet
  • Eliminates manual filling risks, air entrapment, and volume variance

How?

  • Mechanical: Table-lift drive, twist-and-lock piston, sliding tray, PTFE bearings
  • Electronics & Code: Dual stepper motors controlled via Arduino (C++) with auto-homing and retraction
  • Validation: FEA stress modeling, torque testing, and load-cell mass verification

Results

  • 10× faster process (~1 min/syringe) with reduced contamination risk
  • Achieved ±0.05 mL accuracy and ≤1 mL dead volume
  • Adopted by client; co-inventor on patent-pending design package

Pediatric Cerebral Palsy Remote Gait Analysis Device — ESP32, BNO055, FSR, Python, Inventor

IBEHS 3P03: Health Solutions Design Problems · 2024 · Team of 3

What?

  • Wearable monitor to track equinus gait (toe-walking) in pediatric cerebral palsy patients
  • Enables clinicians to evaluate gait progression remotely between clinic visits

How?

  • Sensing: Dual BNO055 IMUs calculate relative tibia-to-foot angle; FSR sensors track heel/toe load distribution
  • Hardware & Software: Custom 3D-printed enclosure; real-time telemetry piped to Python (Matplotlib)

Results

  • Validated dynamic ankle angle and loading curves over 60-second trials
  • Delivered low-cost prototype at ~$162 total BOM cost

SharkSafe — Biomimetic Antibacterial Phone Case — SolidWorks

MDL CADathon · 1st Place · 2025 · Team of 4

What?

  • Phone case featuring a micro-patterned, shark-skin surface that prevents biofilm build-up without chemicals
  • Provides continuous, non-toxic antibacterial surface protection for everyday devices

How?

  • CAD Modeling: Designed 2 µm wide, 0.4 µm high micro-riblets in SolidWorks based on fluid dynamics literature
  • Materials & DFM: Selected flax-reinforced Ecoflex bioplastic molded via laser-ablated tooling

Results

  • Awarded 1st Place at the MDL CADathon
  • Developed commercialization model ($5 unit cost, $25 MSRP) backed by a $50k rollout plan
