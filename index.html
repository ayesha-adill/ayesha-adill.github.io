Pediatric Cerebral Palsy Remote Gait Analysis Device — ESP32, BNO055, FSR, Python, Inventor

IBEHS 3P03: Health Solutions Design Problems · 2024 · Team of 3

What?

  • Built a wearable proof-of-principle device to monitor equinus gait (toe-walking) in children with cerebral palsy
  • Designed so therapists can track gait improvement remotely between clinic visits

How?

  • Used an ESP32 with two BNO055 IMUs to compute the relative tibia-to-foot angle in real time
  • Measured heel and forefoot loading with two force-sensitive resistors, converting readings to approximate force in newtons
  • Designed a custom Inventor housing (breadboard box with slide-on lid and strap handles) and a sock-based sensor mounting concept
  • Streamed data into real-time Python (Matplotlib) plots of angle and force vs. time
  • Met with clinicians to shape how the data is presented

Results

  • Proof-of-principle prototype recorded ankle angle and heel/forefoot loading during 60-second walking trials
  • Defined an analysis approach comparing peak ankle angle per gait cycle against normal gait to track progress
  • Full parts cost of about $162

SharkSafe — Biomimetic Antibacterial Phone Case — SolidWorks

MDL CADathon · 1st Place · 2025 · Team of 4

What?

  • Designed a phone case with a shark-skin-inspired surface that disrupts biofilm formation purely mechanically, with no chemical agents or coatings
  • Targets the problem that phones are touched thousands of times a day and rarely sanitized

How?

  • Modeled the dual-layered micropatterned surface in SolidWorks and researched the most effective dimensions: protruding features with a shallow groove, 2 µm width and spacing, 0.4 µm height
  • Based the design on literature reporting up to ~70% reduction in biofilm formation on shark-skin-inspired topographies
  • Selected a sustainable composite: Ecoflex bioplastic elastomer reinforced with flax straw fibers
  • Planned manufacturing with laser-ablated molds and a two-step injection molding process with a sacrificial film wash-off

Results

  • Won 1st place at the MDL CADathon
  • Built a business case around a $50,000 startup investment and about $5 material cost per case, priced at $25 (base case) and $7 (back panel)
  • Designed to be reusable, compostable, and durable
