Biofill — Automated Syringe-Filling Device — Inventor, Arduino (C++), FEA, Excel, 3D Printing

Capstone Project · 2026 · Team of 4 · Patent Pending

What?

  • Motor-actuated, table-lift syringe-filling machine for Tessella Biosciences
  • Fills 2–10 mL syringes with centrifuged gel inside a biosafety cabinet
  • Replaces a manual process that trapped air bubbles and couldn't scale

How?

  • Mechanical: table-lift drive, twist-and-lock piston, sliding tray, PTFE bearing pads
  • Enclosure: 3D-printed and cleanable for gloved use, with a BOM under $1,500
  • Electronics and code: Arduino C++ for 2 stepper motors, with homing, adjustable volume, and auto piston retraction
  • Validation: torque test rig; accuracy verified by weighing syringes over repeated trials
  • Failure analysis: FEA and testing of piston detachment and lock shear

Results

  • 10× faster (about 1 min per syringe), with lower contamination risk
  • ±0.05 mL accuracy, ≤1 mL dead volume
  • Adopted by the client, with a full engineering package delivered for scale-up
  • Co-inventor on a patent-pending design

Pediatric Cerebral Palsy Remote Gait Analysis Device — ESP32, BNO055, FSR, Python, Inventor

IBEHS 3P03: Health Solutions Design Problems · 2024 · Team of 3

What?

  • Wearable proof-of-concept system to monitor equinus gait (toe-walking) in children with cerebral palsy
  • Allows clinicians to remotely track ankle joint mobility between clinic visits

How?

  • Sensing: dual BNO055 IMUs for real-time tibia-to-foot relative angle tracking
  • Force measurement: force-sensitive resistors (FSRs) at heel and forefoot to detect gait phases
  • Hardware & Software: custom 3D-printed enclosure (ESP32) streaming telemetry to Python (Matplotlib)

Results

  • Captured continuous ankle kinematic and loading data during 60-second trial walks
  • Established quantitative metric comparing peak gait angles against baseline biomechanics
  • Delivered fully functional prototype with a low $162 total BOM cost
